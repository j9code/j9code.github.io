Site Analysis

As an equestrian, I appreciate how land shape,
slope, and drainage affect horse pastures.

As a mapper, I apply that perspective by using
ArcGIS Pro to conduct terrain/hydrology analyses
for local horse farm owners upon request.

Sourcing and integrating USGS 3DEP lidar data
with county parcel data, I create digital elevation models,
slope analyses, and wetness pattern maps
using the Topographic Wetness Index.

I enjoy helping owners make informed decisions
about pasture suitability and drainage.
